# Data contracts

The site consumes only `site/public/data/*.json`, validated against `data/contracts/<name>.schema.json` (JSON Schema 2020-12, generated by `pipelines/contracts.py`, the single source of truth). Every artifact: `schema_version: "v1"`, `generated_at` (fixed stamp so builds are byte-identical), a `tags` map (field group to honesty tag), compact sorted-key JSON, no NaN or infinity (missing is `null`). Each artifact is under 400 KB gzip; sizes are written to `meta.json.artifact_bytes`.

Tags: OBSERVED / DERIVED / PREDICTED / SIMULATED / SYNTHETIC and "/" combinations.

## Parity rule
Every module artifact carries `tolerance {abs, rel}`: a port passes when `|got - want| <= abs + rel*|want|`. Numbers are published rounded half-up to 10 decimals (`floor(x*1e10+0.5)/1e10`). Integers (PRNG outputs, counts) must match exactly. Algorithms the port must implement are specified in `docs/METHODOLOGY.md`; the vectors list inputs, seeds and outputs.

### Scope notes (what is and is not supported)
- DELAY ONE YEAR is measured in calendar years from today. The 8-year window therefore cuts the last project year (project year 8 falls in calendar year 9) for the payback arrays; NPV always uses all 8 project years. Its payback share is thus lower than BUILD NOW by construction of the window, and the recovered share itself is invariant to the delay (numerator and denominator are both discounted one year more).
- Payback is on discounted cash (PV of cash received / PV of investment). An undiscounted payback is not published.
- `median_payback_year` is conditional on paying back within 8 years; the share that never does is `1 - payback_share_8y`. No median is defined when that share exceeds one half is NOT special-cased: the median of the paid-back futures is still reported alongside the share.
- Estimator assumption indexes are an editorial mapping (what each estimator needs), not a testable property; `note` states caveats (e.g. the naive estimator is unbiased only under randomization).
